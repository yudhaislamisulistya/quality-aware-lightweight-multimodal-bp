# AI assistance and accountability

GPT was used to paraphrase and improve wording. OpenAI Codex also assisted Python
implementation of loading/preprocessing, quality features, model and fusion code,
training/evaluation orchestration, tests, and inspection of executed artifacts.
It assisted code that computes metrics and bootstrap intervals, exports numerical
tables, and generates Matplotlib figures and the methodology flowchart. Codex
also assisted manuscript drafting, restructuring, equation formatting, comments,
and preparation of this source release. It would therefore be inaccurate to
describe the recorded assistance as language paraphrasing alone.

Reported experimental values come from executed scripts and saved predictions,
not invented language-model results. No additional physiological observations or
reference BP labels were generated. Controlled waveform perturbations are coded
augmentation/stress tests that preserve the observed BP labels; they are not new
participants or validated real-world artefacts.

The corresponding author, Yudha Islami Sulistya, is designated responsible for
checking AI-assisted code against the protocol, verifying tables/plots against
executed outputs, checking citations and equations, and ensuring that scientific
interpretation and the final manuscript receive human author review. This states
accountability; it does not claim that all coauthors have completed that review.
AI is not an author. The authors must assess journal-policy compatibility of the
full disclosed assistance rather than claim that it was limited to language work.
